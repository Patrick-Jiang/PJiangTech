Common Questions

Everything a growing business needs to know about our IT partnership.

What IT services do you provide for small businesses?

We handle everything from professional email setup (Microsoft 365) and network cabling to managed security and Tier 3 infrastructure support.

Do I need to hire a full-time IT person?

Not necessarily. PJiangTech can provide ongoing support, project delivery, or senior escalation based on your needs, without requiring you to build a full internal IT department.

How do you ensure the work is performed to professional standards?

Project engagements begin with a detailed Statement of Work (SOW) defining the scope, technical requirements, responsibilities, costs, and expected deliverables. Infrastructure changes are documented, tested, and scheduled to minimize disruption.

Can you help with office moves or new setups?

Yes. We handle the physical infrastructure, including running RJ45 cabling, configuring switches, and ensuring your WiFi is enterprise-ready from day one.
